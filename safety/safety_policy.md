# Safety policy

This is a non-clinical educational prototype intended only for supervised bench use.

1. Do not use the platform for diagnosis, treatment decisions, patient monitoring, or any other medical-device function.

2. The open breadboard must not be attached to a person. Human ECG acquisition is outside the permitted use of this educational build.

3. Waveform-acquisition exercises should use a battery-operated or appropriately isolated ECG simulator according to its manufacturer instructions. A calibrated low-voltage source may be used for ADC-characterisation exercises.

4. Power the prototype from a protected battery-derived supply through a regulator set and verified to 3.3 V before modules are connected.

5. The prototype has no documented certified patient-isolation barrier, leakage-current validation, protective enclosure, or validated input-protection network.

6. USB programming, charging, mains-powered computers, oscilloscopes, and other externally powered instruments must be disconnected from the AD8232 lead interface before the simulator lead cable is attached.

7. The AD8232 lead cable must be fully disconnected before programming, serial debugging, charging, or connecting externally powered test equipment.

8. Use only protected batteries and electrically compatible power components. Verify polarity and supply voltage before every session.

9. Inspect breadboard connections, exposed conductors, cable condition, and ground continuity before operation.

10. No IEC 60601-1, IEC 60601-2-47, ISO 14971, medical-device, clinical-performance, or electrical-safety compliance claim is made.

11. Any future work involving human participants or human physiological acquisition would require a separate hardware safety design, appropriate isolation and protection engineering, leakage-current and electrical-safety evaluation, and the relevant ethics and consent procedures before use.
