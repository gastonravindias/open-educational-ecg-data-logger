# Safety policy

This is a non-clinical educational prototype.

1. No diagnosis, treatment decision, patient monitoring or unsupervised human use.
2. Quantitative tests should use an ECG simulator or calibrated signal source.
3. Do not connect a person to an open breadboard.
4. Electrode-connected operation must be battery-only.
5. Disconnect USB, solar charging and all externally powered equipment before electrode connection.
6. Disconnect electrodes before programming or serial debugging.
7. Use only protected batteries and charger-compatible power components.
8. Inspect polarity, rail voltage, exposed conductors and cable strain before every session.
9. No IEC 60601 or medical-device compliance claim is made.
