# Optional solar-extension design basis

The exact nameplate rating of the photographed panel and the original battery/charger purchase records were not retained. Therefore, the article does **not** claim measured solar sufficiency, runtime or charging efficiency.

For reproducible educational reuse, the optional extension uses a transparent conservative design envelope:

- assumed average core load: 0.60 W (design allowance, not a measurement)
- intended demonstration time: 8 h/day
- regulator efficiency: 0.80
- equivalent peak-sun-hours: 4 h/day
- charging-path efficiency: 0.70
- safety factor: 1.30

Daily energy:
`E_load = (0.60 W × 8 h) / 0.80 = 6.0 Wh/day`

Minimum panel power:
`P_panel,min = (6.0 Wh × 1.30) / (4 h × 0.70) = 2.79 W`

Therefore, a **5 V panel rated at least 3 W** is recommended for the optional educational extension, subject to confirmation that the selected charger accepts the panel voltage/current and matches the protected battery configuration.

This is a design recommendation for replication, not a measured result for the original photographed panel.
